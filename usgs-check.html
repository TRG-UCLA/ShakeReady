ShakeReady · a diagnostic page, not part of the site's menu

USGS connection check Version 2 · seven requests

This page asks USGS, from your browser, for the hazard at one site, its disaggregation, and the ground motions its own models give for the controlling earthquake. It shows what comes back. It exists so that ShakeReady's local analysis can be built against real answers from USGS.

The requests go from your browser straight to earthquake.usgs.gov. Nothing is sent anywhere else. There are seven requests, and the whole run takes about two minutes: please leave the page open until the button at the bottom becomes active.

Send that file back, and the tool can be built against it.

Data

Datasets published by the project in open formats, starting with the structure inventory that powers the map. All files live in this site's public repository, so links are stable and versioned; new datasets will be added here as they are released.

DatasetRecordsDescriptionDownload
NBI bridges25,371 California highway bridges from the FHWA National Bridge Inventory (~2016 snapshot): year built and reconstructed, traffic lanes, ADT, construction type, and longest span. A further 60 records without coordinates are included in the CSV only. JSON · CSV
Instrumented buildings55 Buildings with CESMD strong-motion instrumentation: station, stories, plan shape, dimensions, design date, and sensor layout, with links to station pages. GeoJSON
Instrumented non-ductile buildings6 Instrumented buildings of non-ductile concrete construction in Los Angeles, with structural details and instrumentation history. GeoJSON
LA Times concrete buildings1,451 Pre-1976 concrete buildings in Los Angeles identified by the LA Times survey, with addresses and building names. GeoJSON

Sources and currency

Bridge records derive from the FHWA National Bridge Inventory, which publishes updated state files annually; the snapshot here dates from roughly 2016. Building instrumentation details come from the Center for Engineering Strong Motion Data. Please cite the original sources alongside this site when using the data.

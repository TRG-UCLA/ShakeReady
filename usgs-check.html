USGS connection check

This page asks the USGS hazard service for the hazard curves and the disaggregation at one site, from your browser, and shows what comes back. It exists so that ShakeReady's local analysis can be built against real answers from USGS.

The requests go from your browser straight to earthquake.usgs.gov. Nothing is sent anywhere else. A disaggregation can take a minute.

Send that file back, and the tool can be built against it.
